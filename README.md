# 🎓 Grade Calculator - Semester Tracker

A sleek, desktop-based Python application built with **PySide6** to help students track their grades, calculate weighted averages, and figure out exactly what score they need on their final exams to pass their courses. 

Never guess your final grade again. Know exactly what you need!

## ✨ Features

* **📚 Multi-Course Tracking (Tabs):** Add multiple courses and organize them into beautiful, color-coded tabs.
* **💾 Auto-Save Functionality:** Your data is strictly yours. All grades, weights, and course configurations are automatically saved to a local `my_grades.json` file. Close the app anytime without losing progress.
* **🎯 Dynamic "What Do I Need?" Calculator:** Input your current grades and their weights, and the app instantly calculates the exact mathematical score you need on the remaining percentage to hit your target passing grade.
* **🎨 Custom Course Colors:** Personalize each course tab with a custom color picker for a highly visual and organized experience.
* **🌙 Native Dark Mode:** A modern, distraction-free interface that is easy on the eyes during late-night study sessions.
* **⚠️ Error Handling & Alerts:** The app intelligently warns you if your total weights exceed 100% or if passing the course has become mathematically impossible.
